Privacy notice

This notice explains what we do with the personal details you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), postcode and any details you share about the job.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, arrange and invoice the work. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your details. Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, which processes them on our behalf.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer we delete your enquiry within 12 months. Customer records are kept for as long as we're required to for tax and accounting purposes.

Cookies

This website doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@example.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
