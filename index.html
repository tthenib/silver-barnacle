Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you take away garden waste?

Yes. Green waste is removed and disposed of responsibly, and that's included in your quote.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No, as long as we can get into the garden. Lots of regular customers leave a side gate unlocked for us.

Can I book a one-off job?

Of course. We do one-off tidy-ups and clearances as well as regular maintenance visits.

How do I pay?

We invoice after each visit (or monthly for regular customers) and accept bank transfer or card.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop us. If the weather would damage your lawn or beds, we'll rearrange and let you know.
